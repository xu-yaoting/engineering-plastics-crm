# JEKIN CRM

This context defines the customer-development language used across company records, contacts, follow-ups, and downstream sales work.

## Language

**Company**:
The organization being developed, maintained, quoted, or served. It is the primary customer record whether or not it has purchased yet.
_Avoid_: Account, client record, contact company

**Company Public Contact Details**:
The shared channels published for a Company, such as its main phone number, general email address, website, and postal address.
_Avoid_: Contact details

**Contact**:
A person associated with one Company who has a role in communication, evaluation, purchasing, decision-making, finance, or logistics.
_Avoid_: Customer, account

**Contact Direct Details**:
Channels that reach a specific Contact, such as a work email address, direct phone, mobile number, or WeChat ID.
_Avoid_: Company contact details

**Development Stage**:
The Company's current position in the pre-opportunity development journey: Uncontacted, Contacted, Replied, Qualified, Converted, Nurturing, or Stopped.
_Avoid_: Company status, sales stage

**Company Lifecycle Status**:
The Company's broader operating condition, such as Active, On Hold, Dormant, or Blacklisted. It is independent of Development Stage.
_Avoid_: Development stage

**Follow-up**:
A scheduled next action for maintaining or advancing a Company relationship, optionally directed to a specific Contact.
_Avoid_: Reminder, activity

**Follow-up Workbench**:
The cross-company view of overdue, due, upcoming, and unscheduled Follow-ups for customer development and maintenance.
_Avoid_: Sales pipeline, contact list

**Activity**:
A completed interaction or internal record associated with a Company, optionally identifying the Contact involved.
_Avoid_: Follow-up, task

**Converted**:
The Development Stage reached when a Company produces a formal Inquiry and moves into the sales process.
_Avoid_: Won, customer

**Sales Pipeline**:
The post-conversion flow that tracks Inquiries, Quotations, Samples or Testing, and Orders.
_Avoid_: Customer development, follow-up workbench
